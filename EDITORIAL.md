# Editorial standards

## The test

Content belongs in this guide when it helps a startup complete a journey, make a
decision, avoid a difficult-to-reverse mistake, or find the correct source of
support.

Comprehensiveness alone is not a goal.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Current Microsoft Learn, Azure documentation, official Microsoft product
   pages, or official Microsoft GitHub organizations
2. Microsoft employee-authored community content with a clear disclaimer
3. Independent community content with demonstrated technical depth
4. First-hand founder experience, clearly labeled as experience rather than
   product behavior

Technical and program claims must link to an official source whenever one
exists. Community sources may provide implementation detail but must not
override official support, billing, security, compliance, pricing, quota, or
availability guidance.

## Required qualities

- State the intended startup stage and persona.
- Explain the recommendation, tradeoff, and revisit trigger.
- Prefer durable concepts over screenshots of a changing portal.
- Mark Preview features explicitly.
- Avoid exact prices, credit amounts, quotas, and limits unless the value is
  fetched from an official source and the retrieval date is visible.
- Note that public pricing may differ from negotiated agreements.
- Separate facts from opinions.
- Use plain language and define Microsoft-specific terms.
- Never publish credentials, customer data, internal Microsoft information, or
  private support details.

## Resource inclusion

A resource must:

- solve a documented startup problem;
- have a clear owner and accessible URL;
- disclose whether it is official or community-authored;
- show evidence of maintenance or remain conceptually durable;
- avoid undisclosed affiliate or lead-generation intent; and
- add value beyond an existing canonical source.

Resources are removed or archived when they are broken, materially stale,
superseded, unsafe, or no longer useful.

## Tone

Be direct without being defensive. A poor experience is data. Explain the
supported path, acknowledge complexity honestly, and give the reader a
practical next action.

Do not disparage competing clouds or imply that Azure is always the correct
choice. The guide earns trust by making constraints visible.

## Review

Changes involving identity, billing, support, security, program eligibility,
pricing, quotas, or Preview features require an official source and a maintainer
review.
