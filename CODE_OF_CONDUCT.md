# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, assume good intent, and critique ideas and evidence rather than
people. Founders often arrive here after a frustrating experience; dismissing
that experience is not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization maintainers. Maintainers
will review reports promptly and protect the privacy and safety of reporters.
