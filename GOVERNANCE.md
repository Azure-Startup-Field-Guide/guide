# Governance

## Mission

Help startups move from Azure interest to a secure, supportable, cost-aware
production foundation without navigating disconnected documentation alone.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers:

- protect editorial independence;
- require sources for technical and program claims;
- review time-sensitive guidance;
- keep contribution decisions transparent;
- label official and community content accurately; and
- remove unsafe, promotional, or abandoned material.

## Decision model

Routine content changes are approved through pull requests. Material changes to
scope, licensing, governance, branding, or source policy require:

1. a public issue describing the proposal;
2. at least seven days for discussion; and
3. approval from a majority of active maintainers.

Security fixes and removals of harmful guidance may be handled immediately.

The project is currently in a single-maintainer bootstrap phase. Until a second
active maintainer is added, material decisions still require the public issue
and discussion period above, and the sole maintainer records the final decision
with rationale. The maintainer roster should expand after a contributor has
demonstrated sustained review quality across multiple changes.

## Freshness

- Automated link checks run weekly and on pull requests.
- Time-sensitive pages should be reviewed at least quarterly.
- Program, pricing, quota, region, support, and Preview guidance receives
  priority.
- A visible "last reviewed" claim must reflect a substantive source review, not
  an automated timestamp.
- Broken or superseded resources may be removed without preserving list size.

## Conflicts of interest

Contributors must disclose employment, ownership, sponsorship, referral, or
commercial relationships that could influence a recommendation.

Microsoft employees contribute in a personal capacity unless a contribution is
made from an official Microsoft-owned source and explicitly identified as such.

## Maintainer growth

Consistent contributors can be invited as domain reviewers or maintainers.
Maintainer access is based on sustained review quality, not employer or
follower count.
